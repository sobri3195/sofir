# SEO AI Article Generator - Article Types Guide

## Overview

The SOFIR SEO AI Article Generator now supports **5 specialized article types**, each optimized for different content purposes and affiliate marketing strategies. Powered by Google Gemini AI, each article type uses specialized prompts to generate content that matches industry best practices.

## Supported Article Types

### 1. 📝 General Article
**Best For:** Standard informational, educational, and how-to content

**Features:**
- Flexible purpose selection (informational, educational, transactional, how-to)
- Suitable for blog posts, guides, and educational content
- Balanced structure with introduction, body, and conclusion
- FAQ section support
- Table of contents generation

**When to Use:**
- Creating educational blog posts
- Writing how-to guides
- Publishing industry news and insights
- Building thought leadership content

**Example Titles:**
- "How to Improve Your SEO in 2024"
- "Complete Guide to WordPress Security"
- "10 Ways to Boost Your Website Speed"

---

### 2. 🛍️ Product Roundup
**Best For:** Comparing multiple products in the same category

**Features:**
- Product names input (one per line)
- Key features to compare
- Comparison tables
- Individual product sections with pros/cons
- "How We Chose" methodology section
- Buying guide
- Final recommendations for different user types

**Special Fields:**
- **Product Names:** Enter multiple product names (one per line)
- **Product Features:** Key features to highlight (e.g., "Price, Performance, Battery Life")

**Content Structure:**
1. Introduction explaining the roundup
2. Comparison table summarizing all products
3. Detailed section for each product
4. Methodology section
5. Buying guide
6. Final recommendations
7. Conclusion

**When to Use:**
- Affiliate marketing content
- "Best [Product Category] of 2024" articles
- Product comparison guides
- Category buying guides

**Example Titles:**
- "7 Best Wireless Headphones for 2024"
- "Top 10 Gaming Laptops Compared"
- "Best Budget Smartphones: Complete Roundup"

---

### 3. ⭐ Product Review
**Best For:** In-depth analysis of a single product

**Features:**
- Single product name input
- Detailed specifications table
- Pros and cons list
- Performance testing section
- Rating scores
- Competitor comparison
- Purchase recommendation

**Special Fields:**
- **Product Name:** Single product name for review

**Content Structure:**
1. Product introduction and first impressions
2. Detailed specifications
3. Design and build quality
4. Performance testing results
5. Comprehensive pros and cons
6. Comparison with competitors
7. Who should buy this product
8. Final verdict and rating
9. Where to buy (with CTA)

**When to Use:**
- Detailed product reviews
- Affiliate product content
- Expert analysis articles
- Purchase decision guides

**Example Titles:**
- "iPhone 15 Pro Review: Is It Worth the Upgrade?"
- "Sony WH-1000XM5 Complete Review"
- "MacBook Pro M3 Performance Analysis"

---

### 4. ⚖️ Comparison Post
**Best For:** Head-to-head comparison of 2-3 products

**Features:**
- Multiple product names (2-3 products)
- Comparison criteria specification
- Side-by-side comparison tables
- Winner declarations for each criterion
- Final scorecard
- Best choice recommendations

**Special Fields:**
- **Product Names:** Enter 2-3 product names (one per line)
- **Comparison Criteria:** Criteria for comparison (e.g., "Features, Price, Performance")

**Content Structure:**
1. Introduction explaining the comparison
2. Quick overview of each product
3. Side-by-side comparison table
4. Detailed comparison by criteria:
   - Features comparison (winner declared)
   - Performance comparison (winner declared)
   - Price comparison (winner declared)
   - User experience (winner declared)
5. Final verdict and scorecard
6. Recommendations for different user types

**When to Use:**
- Direct product comparisons
- "Product A vs Product B" articles
- Decision-making guides
- Competitive analysis

**Example Titles:**
- "iPhone 15 Pro vs Samsung S24 Ultra: Which Should You Buy?"
- "Notion vs Monday.com: Complete Comparison"
- "MacBook Pro vs Dell XPS 15: Head-to-Head"

---

### 5. 📋 Listicle
**Best For:** Numbered list articles optimized for engagement

**Features:**
- Configurable item count (5-50 items)
- Numbered H2 headings
- Scannable format
- Shareable structure
- Viral-worthy content

**Special Fields:**
- **Number of List Items:** Choose how many items (5-50)

**Content Structure:**
1. Compelling introduction
2. Numbered list items (each with):
   - Catchy subheading
   - Detailed explanation (100-200 words)
   - Supporting details
   - Examples
3. Brief conclusion
4. Call-to-action

**When to Use:**
- Viral content creation
- Quick tips and tricks
- Curated lists
- Social media-friendly content
- Traffic generation

**Example Titles:**
- "15 WordPress Plugins Every Site Needs"
- "20 SEO Tips That Actually Work"
- "10 Email Marketing Mistakes to Avoid"

## How to Use

### Step 1: Access the AI Generator
1. Go to **SOFIR → SEO** in WordPress admin
2. Scroll down to the **AI Article Generator** section
3. Enter your Google Gemini API key (if not already configured)

### Step 2: Select Article Type
1. Click the **Generate Article** tab
2. Choose your desired **Article Type** from the dropdown:
   - General Article
   - Product Roundup
   - Product Review
   - Comparison Post
   - Listicle

### Step 3: Fill in Fields
The form will dynamically show relevant fields based on your article type selection:

**For General Article:**
- Article Title
- Target Keyword
- Article Purpose
- Tone
- Other settings

**For Product Roundup:**
- Article Title
- Target Keyword
- Product Names (one per line)
- Product Features to Compare
- Tone
- Other settings

**For Product Review:**
- Article Title
- Target Keyword
- Product Name
- Tone
- Other settings

**For Comparison Post:**
- Article Title
- Target Keyword
- Product Names (2-3 products)
- Comparison Criteria
- Tone
- Other settings

**For Listicle:**
- Article Title
- Target Keyword
- Number of List Items
- Tone
- Other settings

### Step 4: Configure Settings
Adjust additional settings:
- **Tone:** Professional, Casual, Friendly, etc.
- **Word Count:** 300-5000 words
- **Point of View:** First, Second, or Third person
- **Readability Level:** Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced
- **Creativity Level:** Slider from Conservative to Creative
- **Include FAQ:** Add FAQ section
- **Include TOC:** Add table of contents

### Step 5: Generate
1. Click **Generate Article**
2. Wait for the AI to process (may take 30-60 seconds)
3. Review the generated content in 6 tabs:
   - Content
   - Meta Data
   - Outline
   - Keywords
   - SEO Analysis
   - Schema

### Step 6: Publish
1. Review the SEO score and suggestions
2. Make any necessary edits
3. Click **Save as Draft** or **Publish**
4. The article will be created as a WordPress post with all metadata

## Best Practices

### For Product Roundup
- Include 5-10 products for optimal engagement
- Be specific with product features to compare
- Ensure fair and balanced reviews
- Include affiliate disclosure

### For Product Review
- Be honest about pros and cons
- Include real-world testing insights
- Compare with direct competitors
- Provide clear purchase recommendation

### For Comparison Post
- Limit to 2-3 products for clarity
- Use consistent comparison criteria
- Declare winners objectively
- Help readers make informed decisions

### For Listicle
- Start with most important/interesting items
- Keep items scannable and concise
- Use numbers in title (e.g., "10 Best...")
- Make it shareable on social media

### For All Types
- Always use relevant target keywords
- Review and edit AI-generated content
- Add personal insights and experiences
- Include relevant images
- Update affiliate links
- Add proper disclosures

## Technical Details

### REST API Endpoint
```
POST /wp-json/sofir/v1/seo-ai/generate
```

**Parameters:**
- `title` (required)
- `keyword` (required)
- `article_type` (required): general|product_roundup|product_review|comparison|listicle
- `product_names` (optional): For product-related types
- `product_features` (optional): For product roundup
- `comparison_criteria` (optional): For comparison posts
- `list_count` (optional): For listicles
- `tone`, `word_count`, `pov`, `readability`, `creativity`
- `include_faq`, `include_toc`

### AJAX Action
```javascript
sofir_generate_seo_article
```

## Troubleshooting

### Article Not Generating
- Check that Google Gemini API key is configured
- Ensure you have internet connectivity
- Try reducing word count
- Check error messages in browser console

### Content Quality Issues
- Adjust creativity level
- Be more specific with product names and features
- Provide clearer comparison criteria
- Review and edit generated content

### SEO Score Low
- Review SEO suggestions tab
- Ensure keyword appears in title
- Add meta description
- Include FAQ section
- Use proper heading structure
